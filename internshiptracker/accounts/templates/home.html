GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What is InternTrack?

InternTrack is an internship search organizer designed to help you manage saved jobs, submitted applications, interview progress, and follow-up tasks.

Can I import jobs from LinkedIn?

You can build support for importing available information from compatible exports, such as CSV files. Direct access to saved jobs and applications depends on LinkedIn's current APIs, permissions, and applicable terms. Logging in with LinkedIn alone does not grant access to all your account data.

Can I use other job platforms?

Yes. You can manually record opportunities from other platforms. Additional imports or direct integrations can be added when the provider supports them.

Can I track interviews and follow-ups?

The application is designed to support interview records, recruitment stages, notes, and follow-up dates. These features need to be connected to your Django dashboard and database.

Is my information automatically synchronized?

Only when a supported integration is configured and authorized. File imports and manual entries are alternatives when automatic synchronization is not available.
