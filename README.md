# Internship Tracker
Many university students approaching graduation face the challenge of finding a good job with a competitive salary due to their lack of professional experience. One of the best ways to gain that experience and improve their chances of landing a full-time position is through an internship.

 ## But why choose an internship over a regular job?
Finding an internship that provides valuable knowledge, practical experience, and a flexible schedule can be easier than finding a full-time job that offers the same benefits. Internships allow students to develop their skills, explore different career paths, and prepare for the professional world.

However, once you decide to pursue an internship, you may encounter *another problem*: keeping track of all your applications. You apply to multiple positions on different websites, receive emails from various companies, and try to remember which applications are still pending, which companies have responded, and which opportunities require follow-up. Before you know it, some applications get lost in the process.
## That's where Internship Tracker comes in!
Internship Tracker is a tool designed to help you organize and manage all your internship applications in one place. Instead of switching between multiple job portals, searching through your inbox, or relying on your memory, you can keep everything organized on a single dashboard.

With Internship Tracker, you can easily monitor the status of each application, keep track of important dates, store relevant links and notes, and stay on top of upcoming interviews or follow-up deadlines.

**_Your internship search doesn't have to be chaotic. Stay organized, save time, and focus on what really matters: finding the right opportunity to kick-start your career._**
